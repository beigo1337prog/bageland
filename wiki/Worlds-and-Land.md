## Title screen and worlds

Play opens the Bageland title screen. Behind the menu your last world keeps running (read-only: nothing there is saved), with the camera drifting over it; a first visit shows a small starter factory. The logo is pixel art drawn in code. The game's version is in the bottom-left corner; clicking it opens the changelog.

The menu: **Continue** (the last world you played, with its money and play time), **New world**, **Load world**, **Join co-op**, then a smaller row of **Settings**, **Changelog**, **Leaderboards** and **Invites** (co-op invitations, with Accept and Decline; a banner across the top shows new ones), and **Quit** (not in the browser). With no saved worlds, Continue and Load world are hidden. **Sign in with Discord** is in the top-right corner; once signed in, your picture there opens your **profile**. In **Load world**, co-op worlds have a teal stripe, a **CO-OP** tag with their code and their members' pictures; solo worlds say **SOLO** and have **Make co-op**.

**Profiles:** worlds (and how many are co-op), day streak (and best), active days, play time (and this week), total earned, best leaderboard place, a year of daily activity, every world with its play time, money earned and research, and each leaderboard place. Click anyone on a leaderboard to see theirs, or type a @handle and press Find. Play time counts in any world while you're signed in. The title screen is used with the mouse; Esc closes a page or dialog.

- **New world**: name, **Players** (Solo, or Co-op: the world is made a co-op world as it starts; needs Discord sign-in), **Difficulty**, resources (deposits only, or every resource everywhere), how many deposit patches and how big they are, and starting money (from $500 up to a $10,000,000 sandbox). Every world gets a random seed; **New map**, or a click on the preview, rolls another. A key under the preview lists the deposits it draws. **Create world** starts it.
- **Difficulty** sets the resources and starting money together:

  | Difficulty | Resources | Deposits | Starting money |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Easy | Everywhere | — | $15,000 |
  | Medium | Deposits | Plentiful, large | $15,000 |
  | Hard | Deposits | Normal, normal size | $5,000 |

  Changing any other setting makes the world **Custom**. Only Easy, Medium and Hard worlds go on the leaderboards, each on its own board. **Best income / s** is the best income a second held over 10 minutes of play. A solo world's scores belong to the Discord account that first sent them: a copy opened on another account doesn't rank. Worlds made before difficulties count as whichever one their settings are closest to (sandbox worlds stay Custom).
- **Load world**: every saved world with a little map of its factory, its money, building count, play time, difficulty, size, settings and when it was last played. **Play** (or double-click the world), **Rename** or **Delete** (after a confirm).
- **Settings**: interface size, graphics (Extra low draws everything as plain blocks of colour, otherwise like Low; High; Medium halves the working animations and only draws deposits on your land and plots for sale, capped at 60 fps; Low turns the animations off and only draws deposits on your land, capped at 30 fps; in the browser, Low and Extra low also draw at one pixel per screen point on high-density screens, from the next page load), building labels (names on every building of 2×2 or more), autosave interval (1 to 10 minutes, or off) and fullscreen. They save at once to `settings.json`; interface size and art style apply when a world loads.

In a world, Esc (with nothing selected or being placed) or the menu button at the top left opens the game menu. Its header shows the world's name, a SOLO or CO-OP tag and its difficulty, money and play time. Then: Resume [Esc], Save [F5], Settings [O], Give feedback [J], Host co-op [K], Make co-op world [M] (solo worlds), Save and quit to title [T], Quit game [Q]. The game pauses while it is open. F5 saves at any time. **Give feedback** sends a message (up to 2000 characters, Enter sends) straight to the developer, with the world's name, the game version, its difficulty and a picture of the world (without the menus); you don't need to be signed in.

**Co-op:** Host co-op [K] on a solo world opens a **temporary room** under a new code for this session only; guests play along but keep no copy, and nothing is saved to the server. **Make co-op world [M]** (on that card, in the game menu, or **Make co-op** in Load world; needs Discord sign-in) makes the world a co-op world for good: its 5-letter code is reserved for it, nobody else can host under it, and it can't go back to solo. The game menu says **Solo world**, **Co-op world CODE** or **Co-op guest**. The room code shows in big letters with **Copy link [C]**, **Share [Enter]** and, for a co-op world, **Invite [I]** and the list of members. Invite takes a Discord handle (@name); that player gets the invite as a banner on the title screen and on its **Invites** page, and accepting makes them a member and opens the world. Every member keeps a copy in their own **Load world** list. Anyone with the code can still join live while a member hosts, but only members keep the world or can open it by code when nobody is online. Opening a co-op world joins whoever already has it open; otherwise it opens your copy and hosts its room for the others. The host uploads the world whenever it saves (autosave, F5) and on leaving; while other players are in the room it also uploads every minute and when the tab goes into the background. If the server has a newer copy than yours when you open the world, that copy opens instead. On the leaderboards a co-op world shows once, with all its members' names and pictures, and it also counts for each member. When the host leaves, members keep playing their copy and one of them takes over hosting under the same code; a player who isn't a member goes back to the title screen. Everyone in a room needs the same game version; an older one is asked to update.

## Land

Every new world is 144×144 tiles (81 plots of 16×16; worlds made before v0.73 stay 96×96). You start owning the 3×3 plots round the centre (48×48 tiles; the four centre plots, 32×32, on older 96×96 worlds) and can only build on land you own. Press **P** (or **Land** in the top bar) to open the land tool: plots next to your land are tinted and show the price of the next plot; click one to buy it. The first plot costs $1,000 and plot *k* costs $1,000 × *k*^2.3 (plot 10 about $200k, plot 30 about $2.5M, the last about $22M). Worlds made before v0.73 keep their old prices: each plot 40% more than the last. Unowned land is drawn darker. Right-click or Esc leaves the tool. Worlds saved before plots existed keep their map size and own all of it.
